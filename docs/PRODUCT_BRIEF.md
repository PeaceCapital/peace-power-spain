# Product Brief

## What This Product Is

This is **not just a memo dashboard** and **not just a research notebook**.

The intended product is a **full Iberian power pricer and market-monitoring system**.

Core identity:

`Spain / Iberia day-ahead pricer, signal engine, and technical market monitor`

## Product Pillars

### 1. Pricer

The core engine should price and score:
- OMIE day-ahead power conditions
- sub-thermal and negative-price risk
- storage-adjusted renewable surplus
- congestion-sensitive regime behaviour

Outputs:
- point estimate
- confidence / uncertainty band
- regime score
- negative-price probability
- signal strength

### 2. Signal Engine

The signal layer should transform the pricer into actionable market intelligence.

Core signals:
- RSI after BESS
- thermal-floor discount
- negative-price probability
- congestion pressure / ICS
- storage sunset risk

Outputs:
- signal regime
- entry / no-trade / caution states
- invalidation triggers
- sizing guidance or expression guidance

### 3. Technical Dashboard

The dashboard should become the operational front-end for the system.

Required sections:
- pricing view
- signal monitor
- storage risk monitor
- congestion monitor
- market alerts
- market context / briefing
- eventually live news and event overlays

The dashboard is not only for presentation.
It should become the daily operating screen.

### 4. Alerting Layer

The system should flag:
- negative-price regime risk
- high-conviction short regimes
- storage sunset breaches
- congestion hotspots
- changes in OMIE vs thermal floor
- relevant market/news events once integrated

### 5. Briefing Layer

The eventual front end should also support:
- short daily written market brief
- top signal changes
- major drivers
- relevant Iberian power news
- event / catalyst awareness

## Who It Is For

Primary users:
- bank commodities / structuring desks
- energy-transition advisory teams
- storage / infrastructure investors
- power-market consultants

Secondary users:
- IPPs
- asset optimizers
- merchant-risk teams

## What Makes It Different

The differentiator is not “we also use AI.”

The differentiator is:
- Iberia-specific depth
- OMIE-first pricing logic
- BESS absorption and sunset framing
- REE node-driven congestion layer
- explainable market-structure intelligence
- bank/advisory-friendly outputs

## Build Direction

The project should evolve in this sequence:

1. Research pricer
2. Live data
3. Full pricer outputs
4. Technical dashboard with alerts
5. Market briefing / news overlay
6. Client-facing daily product

## Important Constraint

Do not let the product drift into:
- generic European AI trading platform
- generic energy news dashboard
- pure consulting slideware

The north star remains:

`A technical, explainable Iberian power pricer and market-monitoring product.`
