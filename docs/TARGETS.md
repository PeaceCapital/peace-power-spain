## What To Ask Each Group

### Validators

- Does the OMIE + BESS + congestion framing feel commercially credible?
- What is missing for a real Iberian desk workflow?
- At what point would storage materially kill the short thesis?

### Advisors / Investors

- Would this be useful in storage diligence, power-market diligence, or transition strategy work?
- Would clients pay for this as a recurring signal product or only as project work?

### Banks

- Would a desk use this as market colour, structuring input, or risk monitoring?
- Is the better entry point Commodities, Infrastructure, Sustainable Finance, or Strategic Advisory?
